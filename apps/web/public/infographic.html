Top Takeaways

  • Out-of-box Copilot delivers instant productivity with no development.
  • Declarative agents add domain-specific Q&A using internal knowledge.
  • SharePoint agents turn intranet content into fast, site-specific answers.
  • Copilot Studio integrates external systems and custom workflows.
  • Azure AI Foundry supports fully custom AI apps with maximum flexibility.

Best-Fit Scenarios

  • M365 Copilot: General productivity improvements.
  • Declarative Agents: HR/IT FAQs, onboarding, internal policy bots.
  • SharePoint Agents: Intranet Q&A and content discovery.
  • Copilot Studio: Multi-step workflows and external API integration.
  • Azure Foundry: Bespoke AI apps or customer-facing bots.
